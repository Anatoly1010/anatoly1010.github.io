I went to Yekaterinburg to watch the team at Element get a cryogen-free NMR spectrometer from a Chinese manufacturer up and running. That was the plan, anyway. The first surprise was how much bench you'd need for a benchtop instrument. The second was how much of the setup I'd end up doing myself.

I helped install the software and set up the computer. With no instructions to go on, we pieced together the network settings from the configuration files, then moved one of those files from one folder to another. The software still refused to run. It turned out the computer was using Russian regional settings, which use a comma as the decimal separator. The software insisted on a point. As for how the spectrometer itself performs, it's too early to say.

What stuck with me about Yekaterinburg was how wide the streets were in the centre and how good the city looked after dark. And the taxi from the airport cost me 600 roubles. In Novosibirsk, it was 1,600.
