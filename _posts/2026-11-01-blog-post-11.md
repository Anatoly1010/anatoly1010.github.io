Three papers came out over September and October, all with EPR at their core.

- <a href="/publication/2026-09-27-paper-39">Sterically shielded nitroxide biradicals</a>, in <i>Chemical Physics</i>. Six biradicals on a bicyclic nitroxide core, where different diastereoisomers turn out to have very different exchange coupling. One of them works as a DNP agent, with an enhancement factor of 18 in both <sup>1</sup>H MAS and <sup>1</sup>H → <sup>13</sup>C CP MAS.
- <a href="/publication/2026-10-17-paper-40">Linker effect in TEMPO-based radicals and diradicals</a>, in <i>Russian Chemical Bulletin</i>. How the linker between two TEMPO units sets the exchange and dipolar coupling, from EPR in liquid and frozen solutions, relaxation measurements and quantum chemistry.
- <a href="/publication/2026-10-28-paper-41">A V<sub>22</sub> cyclic coordination cluster</a>, in <i>Polyoxometalates</i>. A giant mixed-valent vanadium(III,IV) ring that, despite its size, has a small triplet ground spin state, and is visible by EPR.

All three are on the <a href="/publications/">Publications</a> page.
